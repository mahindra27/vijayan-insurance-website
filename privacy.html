Privacy Policy

How your personal information is collected, used and protected when you contact me through this website.

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how S. Vijayan Supramaniam ("I", "me", "my"), an authorised representative of Great Eastern Life Assurance (Malaysia) Berhad, handles personal information collected through this website. It is provided in the spirit of Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

1. Information I collect

When you submit the enquiry form, contact me via WhatsApp, phone or email, I may collect:

  • Your name, age, occupation and contact details (phone, WhatsApp, email);
  • Details of your insurance interest and any existing coverage you choose to share;
  • Your preferred consultation time and any message or questions you send.

Providing this information is voluntary. However, without certain details I may be unable to respond to your enquiry effectively.

2. How I use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation;
  • To understand your protection needs and recommend suitable options;
  • To provide ongoing service, policy reviews and follow-up where relevant;
  • To comply with legal, regulatory and insurer requirements.

3. How the enquiry form works

This website's enquiry form does not store data on a server. When you submit it, the details you entered are formatted into a message and opened in WhatsApp on your own device, ready for you to send to me. You remain in control and can review or edit the message before sending.

4. Sharing of information

Your information may be shared with Great Eastern Life Assurance (Malaysia) Berhad where necessary to provide products or services you request, and with parties required by law or regulation. I do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Data protection

I take reasonable steps to keep your personal information secure and to limit access to it. Communications over WhatsApp, email and phone are subject to those providers' own security and privacy practices.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or withdrawal of consent to use your personal information at any time by contacting me using the details below. Some requests may be subject to legal or regulatory retention requirements.

7. Cookies & analytics

This website may use basic analytics to understand general visitor trends. No sensitive personal information is collected for this purpose. You can control cookies through your browser settings.
